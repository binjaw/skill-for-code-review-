What motivates you to join the Global Mentoring Program?

I have been with FEV since June 2022, working closely with customer teams, suppliers and colleagues across several locations. I've built a solid technical base, and I now see that my next step depends on soft skills more than technical ones. I want to communicate with more confidence and clarity, especially with senior stakeholders and customers. I also want to make my contributions more visible, handle pressure and competing priorities better, and grow toward taking more ownership and leadership. A mentor's outside view and experience would help me develop these skills in a focused way.

How do you see the mentor-mentee relationship contributing to your development?

I hope to get honest feedback on how I come across: in meetings, in written communication, and when I raise problems or disagree. I'd value practical advice on presenting ideas convincingly, negotiating priorities, giving and receiving feedback, and building a network inside FEV. I'd also like to learn how my mentor has handled difficult situations and career decisions, so I can apply those lessons myself. Most of all, I want a trusted person to reflect with, and to set a few clear development goals with that we review together.

How often would you like to meet?

Once a month for about 60 minutes, online, with short check-ins in between if needed.

Is there anything else you'd like your mentor to know about you?

I'm calm, reliable and solution-focused. I like to understand the root cause of a problem and I'm comfortable coordinating between different teams to solve it. I tend to be more reserved in large meetings and would like to speak up with more confidence. I value honesty, so direct feedback is very welcome. English is my working language and I'm currently improving my German.
