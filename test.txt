What motivates you to join the Global Mentoring Program?

After four years at FEV, my technical work is in good shape, but I've realised my growth now depends more on how I communicate, present myself and work with people. I'm looking for an outside perspective: someone who doesn't know me or my daily work yet, and who can challenge my thinking and show me how others handle similar situations. I would like to step outside my DevOps bubble, focus on my strengths and personal growth, and connect with colleagues across the global FEV network.

How do you see the mentor-mentee relationship contributing to your development?

I hope to get honest feedback on how I come across: in meetings, with stakeholders, and when I need to raise a problem or say no. I'd like to understand my own habits better, both what helps me and what holds me back. I also want to build more confidence in speaking up and presenting my ideas. Hearing how my mentor has dealt with challenges, career decisions and difficult conversations would help me find my own way. A regular space to pause and reflect, not just deliver, is something I'm really looking forward to.

How often would you like to meet?

Once a month, about 60 minutes, online.

Is there anything else you'd like your mentor to know about you?

I'm calm, reliable and solution-oriented. I enjoy connecting people across teams to solve a problem. I came to Germany from India and have worked across French, German and Indian environments, so I value different perspectives and enjoy learning from them. I tend to stay quiet in larger groups and would like to speak up with more confidence. I appreciate honest, direct feedback.
